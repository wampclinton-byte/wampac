    🌍 Who We Are

    About Wampac

    Papua New Guinea's trusted real estate platform — connecting landlords, tenants, businesses and travellers across the country and beyond.

    🎯 Our Mission

    Wampac was built with a single goal: to make finding and listing rental properties in Papua New Guinea simple, transparent and accessible to everyone. Whether you're a tenant searching for your next home or a landlord wanting to reach more people, Wampac is the platform built for you.

    We believe access to quality housing and commercial space should never be complicated. That's why we've designed every part of our platform to be fast, mobile-friendly and easy to use — even on a slow connection.

    🛠️ Our Services
    🏠
    Residential Rentals
    Browse apartments, houses, rooms and transit housing across all major provinces in PNG and worldwide.
    🏢
    Commercial Spaces
    Find offices, shops, restaurants, medical clinics, warehouses, land and more — all in one place.
    📋
    Landlord Dashboard
    A dedicated portal for landlords to publish listings, manage bookings and communicate with prospective tenants.
    🔍
    Smart Search
    Filter by country, province, suburb, property type, price range and availability — so you find exactly what you need.
    💬
    Direct Messaging
    Tenants can send enquiries directly to landlords, and landlords can message the Wampac team for support.
    🤝 Who We Serve

    Wampac is built for two key groups:

    • 👨‍👩‍👧
      Tenants & Families — People looking for safe, comfortable residential properties across PNG and beyond. Our platform lets you browse listings by location, filter by budget and contact landlords instantly.
    • 🏗️
      Landlords & Property Owners — Whether you own one property or many, Wampac gives you a professional platform to list your spaces, manage enquiries and track bookings — all from your phone.
    💡 Our Values
    • ✅
      Transparency — Every listing shows clear pricing, availability and contact details. No hidden fees, no surprises.
    • 🌐
      Accessibility — Designed to work on any device, including low-end smartphones and slower mobile connections common across PNG.
    • 🔒
      Trust & Safety — Landlords are registered and verified before their listings go live, giving tenants peace of mind.
    • Local First — We are proudly Papua New Guinean-focused, with deep knowledge of the local housing market and the communities we serve.
